# 🧠 Wings AI + NotebookLM Optimization Strategy

Since Wings AI uses a **"Business Context"** field to guide Gemini's behavior, NotebookLM is your most powerful tool for "training" the AI without writing a single line of code.

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: Data Ingestion (The "Brain" Dump)

Upload the following varied resources into a single NotebookLM notebook:

| Resource Type | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Service Menu / Price List** | Ensures AI never hallucinates prices or service names. |
| **Customer FAQs** | Teaches it the best way to handle common objections or questions. |
| **"Brand Voice" Docs** | Feed it old posts, marketing slogans, or core values (e.g., *"Ân Cần - Chân Thành"*). |
| **Winning Chat Logs** | Export your best customer interactions (text only). This teaches the AI exactly how you talk. |
| **Technical Specs** | Information on glue types, lash curls, and retention periods. |

---

## 🪄 Phase 2: The "Master Context" Generator

Once your data is in NotebookLM, use these prompts to generate the **Business Context** for Wings AI:

### Recommendation 1: The Brand Persona
> *"Based on all uploaded sources, write a 5-paragraph 'Rules of Engagement' for an AI assistant. Focus on the tone (warm/professional), specific Vietnamese phrasing to use, and how to handle pricing questions."*

### Recommendation 2: The Logic Engine (Handling Objections)
> *"Create a concise guide on how to handle customers who think the price is too high or are worried about lash damage, based on our salon's philosophy."*

---

## 🛠️ Phase 3: Bringing it into Wings AI

NotebookLM will give you a very sophisticated summary. Here is how to apply it:

1.  **Summarize**: Ask NotebookLM: *"Synthesize all of this into a 1500-character description suitable for a 'System Instruction' field for a support AI."*
2.  **Paste**: Click the Wings AI icon in your browser (the setup popup).
3.  **Update Business Context**: Paste that synthesis directly into the **"Business Context"** field.
4.  **Save**: Your Wings AI is now "trained" on your entire business history.

---

## 💡 Pro Tip: The "Reverse Training" Loop

Every month:
1.  **Collect**: Save a few chat logs where Wings AI did a great job (and a few where it failed).
2.  **Upload**: Put them back into NotebookLM.
3.  **Refine**: Ask NotebookLM: *"What did the AI miss in these failed examples compared to our rules? Update the 'Business Context' to prevent these mistakes."*

---

## 💎 The "10-Year Deep Learning" Plan

With 10 years of data across multiple roles (Consultants, Technicians, Support) and audio recordings, you are sitting on a goldmine. Here is how to process it:

### 1. The Audio Pipeline (Transcripts)
Don't upload audio files. Use an AI transcription tool (like Whisper or Google Speech-to-Text) to turn those recordings into text.
- **Tip**: Focus on the **Consultant-to-Client** conversations. These are the most valuable for "selling" and "closing" styles.

### 2. Role-Based Synthesis
NotebookLM can handle large amounts of data, but it's better to categorize them first:
- **Consultant Notes**: Use these to craft the "First Impression" and "Sales" rules.
- **Technician Notes**: Use these to create "Technical Troubleshooting" rules (e.g., why lashes fall out early).
- **Support Notes**: Use these for "Conflict Resolution" and "Refund/Correction" policies.

### 3. The "Legacy" Extraction Prompt
Once uploaded, ask NotebookLM:
> *"Analyze 10 years of technician and consultant notes. What are the top 5 most frequent client concerns, and what specific phrases did our best performers use to resolve them? Format this as a 'Communication Cheat Sheet' for the AI."*

### 4. Continuous Refinement (The 80/20 Rule)
You don't need to upload *everything* at once. Start with the last **2 years** of data first. Older data (from 2014-2016) might have outdated pricing or styles.
- **Focus**: The 20% of your data that generated 80% of your best reviews.
